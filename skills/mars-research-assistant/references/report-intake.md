# Report Intake

For a report, link, PDF, excerpt, or screenshot:

1. identify source, author, date, target, horizon, and access limits;
2. separate the report's claims from verified primary facts;
3. steelman the thesis and strongest counter-thesis;
4. record claim status, evidence quality, relevance, and the next verification;
5. state the impact on the current plan or keep it watch-only.

Do not claim to have read inaccessible material. Do not reproduce paywalled or
copyrighted text beyond a short compliant excerpt. User-provided text may be
analyzed but still requires fact verification when it changes a decision.
